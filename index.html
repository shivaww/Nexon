There's more.

Nexon is full of features — live web search with sources, deep research, agentic file access with permission prompts, study mode, and artifacts that render properly. Every one is documented with real screenshots.

It's free today with your own API keys. Managed plans — Go, Plus, Pro, and Max, with credits and daily usage limits — are coming, and your chats stay on your device either way.

All of it is built by a four-person team in Hyderabad, India, shipping from phones in Termux.

Why Nexon exists.

Most AI coding tools need a laptop and a cloud connection. Nexon gives you full agentic coding, file editing, and deep research directly on your Android phone using Termux — with your own API keys, on hardware you own.

$ claude --status

Built on Claude.

Nexon is developed with heavy use of Claude, and it supports Claude properly: prompt caching and extended thinking are implemented natively, and model lists are fetched live, so new Claude releases work the day they ship. When subscriptions launch, managed plans will offer Claude and other models as credits with daily usage limits — no API keys to juggle, and chats still on your device. Your own Claude key works today, free.
