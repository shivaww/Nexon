Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Nexon is an open-source Android app with a local Python bridge that runs inside Termux on your phone. This page explains what data the app handles and who can see it.

What stays on your device

Your chats, settings, research evidence, and the API keys you enter are stored on your phone. Nexon does not sell your data, show ads, or build advertising profiles.

Nexon includes no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

In the default bring-your-own-key mode, your conversations and API keys are stored only on your phone. If you later use the optional Nexon Pro subscription, your requests pass through a small Nexon-run backend that meters credits and forwards them to the model provider. That backend stores credit balances and usage counts — not conversations, and not API keys.

Services you choose to connect

Nexon sends data to outside services only when you set them up. Each one has its own terms and privacy policy.

  • AI model providers. Your prompts, and any file contents or command output the agent includes, go to the provider you pick (for example Anthropic's Claude API) using your own API key. The provider's policy applies to that data.
  • Web search. Search queries are sent to Tavily to fetch live results.
  • Google Drive backup (optional). If you turn it on, Nexon bundles your chats, settings, provider API keys, and artifacts into one file, encrypts it with a key kept in your device's secure storage, and saves it to your own Google Drive as nexon_backup.enc (up to 5 recent versions). It is stored in your Google account and is not sent to Nexon.
  • Sign-in and token refresh. Nexon uses Supabase for authentication and to refresh Google Drive access tokens.
  • Update checks. The app periodically contacts a small Nexon-run server (falling back to GitHub) to check for new versions. No account data is sent.

Agent actions on your device

Nexon agents can run shell commands and edit files in Termux. Every tool call goes through a permission guard you control (ask, session, always, or never). Commands and file contents the agent reads may be sent to your chosen AI provider as part of the conversation.

Your choices

  • Delete chats and settings by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.
  • Remove your API keys at any time from the app's settings.
  • Delete the Drive backup file from your Google Drive.

Children

Nexon is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and date will be posted on this page.
